What is Beyer Hack Club?

Beyer Hack Club is a programming club with the goal of sharing knowledge and collaborating on projects! People will work both club activities as well as personal/group projects!

What is the Hack Club Organization?

Hack Club is a non profit organizations which provides resources for programming clubs, such as fundraising tools, activities, and platforms to share your code! Beyer Hack Club is a Hack Club chapter.
You can find more information here.

When will meetings be?

Meetings will be every Tuesday after school for 60 - 90 minutes in the robotics shop!
